On October 23–24, the Open Source Program Office (OSPO) and DataLab at UC Davis's Shields Library cohosted a ["Reboot the Earth" hackathon](https://unite.un.org/en/reboot-earth) with the United Nations. UC Davis is the third UC campus to host the event, following UC Santa Cruz (fall 2025) and UC San Diego (spring 2026). After a welcome from Omar Mohsine of the UN, the FireForm team, the Santa Cruz winners, called in for an "Ask Me Anything" session to tell students what they got out of the hackathon, what it was like, and how they've kept building. FireForm is now a [Digital Public Good](https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/r/fireform) and is about to ship its first stable release!

Nearly 70 students from at least three universities—many of them meeting for the first time—worked together in fourteen teams to build solutions for one of three climate-related challenges. The winning team's prize is a trip to [UN Open Source Week](https://www.unopensource.org) in New York! There, the team will join winners from other Reboot the Earth hackathons around the world and share their work with the global open source community.

## Fourteen teams, three challenges

Two challenges came from the Davis community and one from the UN:

- "Seeing the Forest for the Trees for Fire Management," from Derek Young of UC Davis and the [Open Forest Observatory](https://openforestobservatory.org)
- "Counting the Cold for California Growers," from Julia Stover and Carson Black of the [UC Davis Fruit & Nut Research & Information Center](https://fruitsandnuts.ucdavis.edu/) and Andy Lyons of [UC Agriculture and Natural Resources](https://ucanr.edu/) (UC ANR)
- "Soil Intelligence," from the UN's [Food and Agriculture Organization](https://www.fao.org/home/en) (FAO)

Mentors and judges from CAL FIRE, UC Davis, UC ANR, and tech companies such as Meta, AWS, and Walmart Global Tech gave students invaluable perspective and feedback throughout the weekend.

## Winning projects

"The Gummybears" won first place with TreeSearch, an interactive map for exploring Open Forest Observatory plot, tree, and drone data by wildfire hazard, plus a forum that connects people working on the same ground. Try the [TreeSearch app](https://treesearch-un-reboot-the-earth-hackathon-2026.streamlit.app/) or explore [TreeSearch's source code on GitHub](https://github.com/Reboot-The-Earth-GummyBears/UN-Reboot-The-Earth-Hackathon).
