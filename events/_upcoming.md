#### All-Campus Virtual OSPO Meetup

**When**: Thursday, November 5, 2026, 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM Pacific

Thinking about open-sourcing your research software but unsure how to approach your Tech Transfer Office (TTO)? Steven Tudor, Director of the Technology Transfer Office at UC Davis, and Mike Cohen, Director of Innovation Ecosystem Development in UC Berkeley's TTO, join us to talk about what their offices actually do when a researcher wants to release code as open source, the worries researchers bring to them most often, and what information the TTO needs to help researchers make decisions. We'll also talk about how your campus OSPO can serve as a first stop on the way to tech transfer, so you can feel informed and confident when you meet with the TTO.

Guest speakers:

- Steven Tudor is Director of the TTO at UC Davis, where he leads the university’s intellectual property management and commercialization activities. His work focuses on helping researchers navigate intellectual property, licensing, and commercialization while supporting practical pathways for moving university innovations into broader use.
- Mike Cohen is Director of Innovation Ecosystem Development in UC Berkeley's TTO, where he drives initiatives and license agreements that catalyze the commercialization of UC Berkeley innovations and support the funding of campus research. In 2019, he led the development of UC Berkeley's Open Source Software Startup (OS3) Good-Standing Program, which enables the university to obtain stock equity in startups that spin out of the university leveraging software developed at, and open-sourced by, the university.

Students, staff, faculty, researchers, and folks outside UC all welcome!

#### All-Campus Virtual OSPO Meetup (February 4, 2027)

**When**: Thursday, February 4, 2027, 11:30 PM – Friday, February 5, 2027, 12:30 AM Pacific

A monthly gathering for the UC open source community. Each meetup is co-hosted by a different campus in the network—the hosting campus sets the agenda for the first half (demos, workshops, guest speakers, whatever they want to share), and the second half is an open community call for network updates, project sharing, and Q&A.
Students, staff, faculty, researchers, and folks outside UC all welcome!
