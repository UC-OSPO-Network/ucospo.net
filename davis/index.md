The UC Davis OSPO is based at the [UC Davis Library](https://library.ucdavis.edu/) and coordinates UC Davis's participation in the UC OSPO Network.

UC Davis is the lead campus for the Network: it holds the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation grant that established the collaboration across six UC campuses, and it hosts the Network's community manager.
