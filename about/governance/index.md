The Network consists of groups that each have an **aim (goal)** and a **domain (scope of authority)**:

- The [Working Groups](../get-involved/working-groups/index.md) each work to achieve a particular goal that will advance the Network's mission
- The Alignment Group facilitates communication within the Network to promote coherence and prevent duplication of work. The Alignment Group meets on the **fourth Wednesday of each month, 11am–12pm Pacific**, and all are welcome to attend! The next meeting is on the [events page](../../events/index.md#alignment-group-meeting).
- The Stewardship Group looks after the Network's long-term health, budget and strategy. The [Stewardship Group publishes summaries](stewardship-summaries/index.md) of its monthly closed-session meetings.

Our full decision and authority domain structure is set out in our [governance model](model.md).
