title: "Stewardship Group Summaries"
---

The Stewardship Group is the UC OSPO Network's leadership body: one or two stewards from each member institution, looking after the Network's long-term health, budget, strategy and vision. Bylaws-level decisions are made here, and the group's full remit is set out in the [governance model](../model.md#stewardship-group).

After each meeting the group publishes a short summary and its key decisions. Full notes stay private and the Chatham House Rule applies, so nothing here is attributed to a person or a campus.
