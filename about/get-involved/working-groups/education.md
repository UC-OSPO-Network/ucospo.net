The Education Working Group builds a connected open source learning culture across UC campuses. It coordinates training through the Network and partners with UC Carpentries, connecting learners and instructors to lessons that already exist and developing new lessons collaboratively where real gaps remain.

**Chair:** Tim Dennis

**Membership:** Open to anyone interested.

## What this group does

The group curates and points to [existing open source lessons](https://uc-ospo-network.github.io/education/), develops new ones where there are real gaps, and coordinates cross-campus training, much of it through the UC Carpentries partnership.

See also the [Education guiding theme](../../guiding-themes/education.md).
