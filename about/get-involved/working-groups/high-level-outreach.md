The High Level Outreach Working Group focuses on strategy and support for outreach to senior leadership, aimed at improving the sustainability of the Network and of campus OSPOs.

**Chair:** Stephanie Lieggi

**Membership:** Open to anyone interested.

## What this group does

The group develops the approach for engaging senior campus leaders, such as chancellors, vice chancellors, and CIOs, and supports the network and its campuses in making the case for sustained OSPO investment.
