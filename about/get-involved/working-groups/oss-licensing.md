The OSS Licensing Working Group clarifies and, where possible, simplifies the rules and procedures around open source licensing at UC, and shares that guidance in a user-friendly, accessible way.

**Chair:** Karla Padilla

**Membership:** Open to participants with use cases or expertise around open source licensing.

## What this group does

Members work through common UC licensing questions and compliance scenarios and turn them into clear, reusable guidance for UC open source projects.

See also the [license template guide](../../../oss-resources/template-guides/license-guide.md).
